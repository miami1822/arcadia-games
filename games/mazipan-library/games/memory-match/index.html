How to Play

Goal

Flip two cards at a time to find matching emoji pairs. Match all pairs to win! Try to do it in the fewest moves possible.

Emoji Sets

  • Animals — farm & wild animals
  • Food — fruits, snacks & meals
  • Sports — sport & activity icons
  • Nature — flowers, plants & weather
  • Faces — emoji expressions
  • Things — objects, music & games

Difficulty

  • Easy — 4×4 grid, 8 pairs (16 cards)
  • Medium — 5×4 grid, 10 pairs (20 cards)
  • Hard — 6×4 grid, 12 pairs (24 cards)

Scoring

  • Each pair of flips = 1 move
  • Fewer moves = better score
  • Best (lowest) moves per difficulty saved automatically
FAQ

What happens when two cards don't match?

Both cards flip face-down again after a short pause. Try to memorise their positions!

Is my best score saved?

Yes — the fewest moves you've used per difficulty is saved in your browser.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! Just tap the cards to flip them. The grid automatically adjusts to your screen size.
