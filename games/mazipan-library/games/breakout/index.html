How to Play

Goal

Bounce the ball with your paddle to break all the bricks. Clear all 8 levels to win!

Controls

  • Mouse — move cursor over canvas to position paddle (desktop)
  • Finger drag — drag left/right on the canvas (mobile)
  • Hold ← → — hold arrow keys for continuous paddle movement

Levels & Patterns

  • 8 levels — each uses a unique brick layout
  • Patterns: Classic, Pyramid, Diamond, Checkerboard, Cross, Fortress, Arrow, Wave
  • Ball speeds up each level
  • Darker bricks take 2 hits to break

Scoring

  • Each brick = 10 × level points
  • 3 lives — ball resets when it falls
  • Best score saved in your browser
FAQ

Why does the ball angle change when hitting the paddle?

The ball reflects based on where it hits the paddle — hitting the edge sends it at a sharp angle, hitting the centre bounces it straight up.

Do bricks reset when I lose a life?

No — your brick progress is saved between lives. Only the ball and paddle reset position.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — automatically saved in your browser's local storage.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! The game scales to fit your screen. Drag your finger across the canvas to move the paddle.
