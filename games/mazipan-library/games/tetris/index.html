How to Play

Goal

Stack falling tetrominoes (shaped blocks) to form complete horizontal lines. Completed lines are cleared and you earn points. The game ends when pieces reach the top.

Controls — Desktop

  • ← → — move piece left/right
  • ↑ — rotate piece
  • ↓ — soft drop (move down faster)
  • Space — hard drop (instant drop)

Controls — Mobile

  • Use the D-pad buttons that appear below the board
  • ↻ = rotate · ▼ = soft drop · ⬇ Hard Drop = instant drop

Levels & Scoring

  • Level increases every 10 lines cleared
  • Pieces fall faster each level
  • Clearing multiple lines at once earns bonus points
  • The ghost piece (transparent) shows where the piece will land
FAQ

What is the ghost piece?

The transparent/faded piece shows exactly where your current piece will land if you drop it now. Use it to plan placements accurately.

How do I score more points?

Clear multiple lines at once — a Tetris (4 lines at once) gives the most points. Higher levels also multiply your score.

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your best score is saved in your browser's local storage.
