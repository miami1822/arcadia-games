How to Play

Goal

Reveal the hidden word by guessing letters. You have 6 wrong guesses before the hangman is complete.

Controls

  • Click / Tap any letter button on screen
  • Keyboard — just type letters directly

Difficulty

  • Easy — short words (4–6 letters)
  • Medium — mid-length words (7–9 letters)
  • Hard — long words (10+ letters)

Scoring

Win words to build a streak. Your longest streak is saved as your best score.

FAQ

Is my best score saved?

Yes — your longest winning streak is saved in your browser's local storage.

What happens to my streak when I lose?

Losing resets the current streak to zero. Click "New Word" to start a fresh run.

Can I change the category mid-game?

Category and difficulty can be changed from the start screen. Each "New Word" continues with the same settings.

Can I type letters on my keyboard?

Yes! Just press any letter key while the game is active and it counts as your guess.
