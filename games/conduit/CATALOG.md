**Conduit** — A calm circuit puzzle. Rotate the tiles to carry power from the teal source to every lamp, using as few moves as you can. 30 levels that grow from 3x3 to 8x8, plus a new daily puzzle every day.
Controls: click/tap turns a tile clockwise; right-click, Shift-click or Q turns it back. Keyboard: arrows move, Space turns, R resets, Esc pauses, G fullscreen.
