**Deadlight** — 3D horror. The power is out and something is walking the halls. Restore the fuses in five ever-changing buildings, manage your flashlight battery, and get out before it finds you. Beat it to unlock Nightmare.
Controls: WASD/arrows move, mouse look, Shift run, F or click flashlight, Esc/P pause, G fullscreen. Touch: left thumb move, right thumb look, on-screen Run/Light buttons.
